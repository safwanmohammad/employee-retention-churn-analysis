# Business Problem

## Overview

Organizations need to understand both the external job market and internal employee retention to improve workforce planning and hiring decisions.

This project analyzes the employment lifecycle across three areas:

1. Job Market Demand
2. Recruitment Performance
3. Employee Retention and Churn

The objective is to transform raw employment and recruitment data into actionable business insights using Python, PostgreSQL, Advanced SQL, and Power BI.

---

## Business Objectives

### 1. Job Market Analysis

Understand:

- Which industries have the highest job demand
- Which job roles are most frequently posted
- Which countries and cities have higher job concentration
- Which skills and technology stacks are commonly required
- How remote work availability varies
- Which experience levels are most demanded
- How employment types differ across industries

### 2. Recruitment Analysis

Evaluate:

- Applicant volume
- Offer generation
- Offer acceptance
- Recruitment conversion
- Recruiter response time
- Recruitment efficiency
- Hiring performance across industries and roles

### 3. Employee Retention Analysis

Identify:

- Overall employee churn
- Churn across industries
- Churn across job roles
- Churn across salary groups
- Churn across experience levels
- Churn across tenure groups
- Employee segments with comparatively higher observed churn

---

## Key Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

- Which industries have the highest demand?
- Which job roles receive the most applicants?
- Which industries have better recruitment performance?
- Does recruiter response time relate to offer acceptance?
- Which employee groups have higher observed churn?
- Does churn vary by salary level?
- Does employee tenure relate to churn?
- Which combinations of employee characteristics show higher observed churn?

---

## Expected Business Value

The analysis can help organizations:

- Understand job market demand
- Improve recruitment planning
- Identify recruitment bottlenecks
- Identify employee groups requiring further retention investigation
- Support workforce planning
- Prioritize areas for deeper HR analysis

> **Important:** The analysis identifies associations and patterns in the available dataset. It does not establish causal relationships between employee characteristics and churn.
